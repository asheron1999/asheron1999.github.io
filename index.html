Heritage

Your people train you in one or two skills at no cost. Everyone is also trained in Jump, Run, Magic Defense, and Loyalty for free.

Class

Each preset spends all 330 attribute points and all 50 skill credits, and its skill list changes with your heritage.

Attributes

Six attributes share 330 points. Each starts at 10 and tops out at 100. Health is half your Endurance, Stamina equals it, and Mana equals Self.

Skills

Training adds 5 to a skill, specializing adds 10 more and makes it rise faster with use. You can only specialize now, during creation.

The class templates — attribute spreads and per-heritage skill lists — along with the skill costs, formulas, and training bonuses are taken from chapter 2 and Table 2.1 of Asheron's Call Official Strategies & Secrets by Rusel DeMaria (Sybex Inc., November 1999, 344 pages, ISBN 0782126545). The 330-point attribute spread and the four skill classes are corroborated by the boxed game manual.

    
Two gaps in the source, left as printed rather than patched over: the guide omits Unarmed Combat from Table 2.1, so its 6 / 6 cost comes from outside the book; and the Aluvian warrior list spends only 38 of its 50 credits, so that preset opens with 12 unspent.
